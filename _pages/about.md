I am Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/) at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://hkust.edu.hk/), supervised by [Professor Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024, where I previously conducted research under Professor Junxian He.

## Academic Background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science** (2024 - Present), Hong Kong University of Science and Technology
  - Supervisor: Professor Junxian He
  - Research Group: HKUST NLP Group

- **B.Eng.** (2020 - 2024), Shanghai Jiao Tong University
  - Received Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship

## Research Interests & Skills

My research focuses on **Natural Language Processing** and **Machine Learning**, with specific interests in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
